The Obelisk

The whole shape of the book, one facet at a time.

0 of 99 facets lit. The rest are cuts in the stone, waiting.

How to read the shard

  • Lit facet. The chapter is out; choose it to read.
  • Bright core. You have finished that chapter.
  • Marked. Where you left off.
  • Dark cut. Not released yet.

Tab once to reach the shard, then use the arrow keys to move between facets. Home and End jump to the first and last. Enter or Space opens the chapter. Nothing here needs a hold, a drag, or a steady hand.
